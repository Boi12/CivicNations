# Civic Nations 1.0 roadmap

Civic Nations remains one NeoForge mod and one JAR. The source is organized into internal systems rather than separate dependent mods.

## Existing foundation

- Persistent nations and membership
- FTB Teams and FTB Chunks integration
- Leader, Treasurer, Official, and Citizen roles
- Nation ledger, history, citizens, banner, treasury, and currency foundation
- Coin Press and Coin Mint Alpha 5 source
- Fixed output name: `civicnations-neoforge-1.20.1.jar`

## Release systems

1. Stabilize and compile the imported Alpha 5 source.
2. Finish Coin Press processing, animation, standard item handling, and Create-compatible logistics.
3. Finish manually authorized Coin Mint cycles and issuance auditing.
4. Add one-currency Coin Purse storage that preserves exact denominations.
5. Add personal and business plots inside national claims.
6. Add player market stalls, buy/sell listings, physical stock, and digital or physical payments.
7. Add the Treasury Exchange Desk and solid 3x3x3 Bank Vault.
8. Store up to 256,000 coins per denomination per currency in each vault.
9. Add withdrawal-only wireless ATMs with configurable per-player daily limits.
10. Add migration, recovery commands, duplication safeguards, configuration, JEI support, tests, and release documentation.
